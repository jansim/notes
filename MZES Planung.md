If you would like to start a new project at the MZES, please s**end us a short email no later than 5 February 2025,** informing us about your request to include a project in the new research programme and your intention to prepare a proposal.  
  
This request should include the **title and a short abstract as well as information on the probable duration, the envisaged required start-up financing, and the financial details (the funding programme and extent of the requested support)**. The Executive Board can then screen the pre-proposals on 12 February and assign them to the research areas.  
  
Please submit the final project proposals for the Advisory Board in English (4 to 6 pages) by email ([mzes-direktorat@uni-mannheim.de](mailto:mzes-direktorat@uni-mannheim.de) [<mailto:mzes-direktorat@uni-mannheim.de>](mailto:mzes-direktorat@uni-mannheim.de)) no later than 12 March 2025. The Executive Board will screen the proposals on 19 March 2025, and forward them to the Advisory Board.
