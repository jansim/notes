**STUDY: Can you tell when music is AI-generated?**


Dear colleagues, dear students,

Do you think you can tell whether music is AI-generated or not? Or are you just interested in listening to some new music? If you answered yes to either of those two questions, you're in luck! In our latest citizen science study we partnered with researchers from Yale to build a little game where you can showcase your listening skills and fulfil your curiosity.

**What?** Online game where you guess whether a song is AI-generated or not, with additional questions relevant for research.
**How long?** About 7.5 minutes.
**Where?** Just click [this link](https://www.themusiclab.org/quizzes/dafi?src=lmu-nl) to play the game.
